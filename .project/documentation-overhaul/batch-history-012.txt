We also draw clear boundaries: where categorical formalism helps (composable pipelines, algebraic rule systems, round-trip contracts) and where it hurts (dynamic typing, actor state, simple CRUD). The conclusion is pragmatic: use the structure, not the vocabulary. Fix the laws, not the names.
429: The path forward is conservative: test the laws we already obey, fix the three impurities that cause real bugs, decompose the monoliths into composable pipelines. The total investment is 12-19 engineering days for Phases 0-1.
